`AutoDeployMCV=Yes` deploys each house's starting base unit as the match opens, computer
houses included, so play begins with construction yards rather than vehicles. Ground that will
not take the building leaves the base unit standing rather than losing it.

Rampastring is credited for the ts-patches patch this follows and CCHyper and tomsons26 for
the Vinifera option.
