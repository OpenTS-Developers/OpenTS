The mouse wheel over the tactical view now zooms the battlefield in whole steps from 1x to 4x, as far as the game resolution allows, while the sidebar and the tab strip keep their size. The wheel used to scroll the sidebar wherever the pointer was; it now does so over the sidebar and the tab strip, or while battlefield zoom is unavailable.

The zoom follows the battlefield-only zoom of the TI Chilong Edition build of Vinifera.
