---
title: Act on both clicks of a double click on the map
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [ZivDero]
---

Since 0.1.0, the map ignored the second click of a double click, so double-clicking a unit or building only selected it. The map now acts on both clicks, as in the original game. Double-clicking a deployable unit selects and deploys it, and double-clicking one of several factories of the same kind makes it the primary factory.
