category: fix
release: 0.1.0
targets: []
credit: [ZivDero]
---

Ordering something a house cannot yet afford now starts its build clock, which then creeps forward as money arrives. Production began only if the house could pay the first installment, and an order placed while short of funds stayed suspended even after the money came in, so the player had to notice and click the icon again.
