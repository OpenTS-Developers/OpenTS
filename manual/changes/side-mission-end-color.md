---
title: Sides choose the mission-end caption color
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: ScreenTextColor
  effect: added
credit: 
  - JoyfulShush
  - Rampastring
---

A side sets `ScreenTextColor=` in the `rules.ini` section named after it to color the large caption that ends a mission or match based on the player's side. The color comes from the side of the player's own house, so in a skirmish or multiplayer game each player sees their own side's color, whichever side won. The black shadow behind the lettering is unchanged.

Credits to Rampastring for their original Vinifera implementation for this feature.
