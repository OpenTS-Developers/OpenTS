---
command_id: ChatToAllies
---

Opens the message editor addressed to the sender's allies, or to the other observers when the sender is an observer. The editor opens only in a game against other machines and only while no line is being edited. A defeated player given the whole map cannot open it, but [coach mode](/systems/observers/#coach-mode) withholds the map and leaves the line open. Backspace is the key it takes by default, unless [`KEYBOARD.INI`](/formats/keyboard-ini/) gives the command another key or gives Backspace to another command. [In-game chat](/systems/chat/) says who receives the line.
