F6 shows the frame benchmark window and hides it again on the next press. The window's own close button hides it as well.

F6 works in play, while a menu screen or one of its controls has focus, and while the window itself has the keyboard. Neither the press nor the release reaches the game or the screen.

[Developer mode and diagnostics](/systems/developer-mode/) covers what the window reports and how to arm the debug keys.
