---
command_id: HealthFilter
---

Sorts the selected objects the player controls into three condition bands: red, at or below [`ConditionRed`](/keys/conditionred/); yellow, at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/); and green above that. On a mixed selection the first press keeps only the most damaged band present and remembers the whole selection. Each further press moves to the next band of that remembered group, in the order red, yellow, green, wrapping from green back to red and skipping any band the group has no units in. The cycle continues while the selection still matches the group, one of the group's bands, or two of them together. Two bands showing is the exception: the press shows the third band, and changes nothing when the group has no units in it. The bands are worked out again on every press, so an object that has been repaired or damaged since moves with its current condition. A selection that matches none of those starts a new filter. It keeps its own most damaged band and replaces the remembered group. The one case that changes nothing is a single band the group does not contain, since there is nothing to remove from it. [`HealthFilterAddLower`](/commands/healthfilteraddlower/) adds the band a `HealthFilter` press would show next, instead of replacing the selection. The remembered group is forgotten when the game clears the scenario, which happens before a scenario or a saved game is loaded, and neither command acts while a structure is being placed.
