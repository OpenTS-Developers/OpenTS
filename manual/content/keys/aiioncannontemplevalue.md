The rating is the sixth of the seven structure tests, so only a structure that is neither a factory, a net power producer, a base defense nor a plug reaches it. Like every list in this family it is read at the firing house's own difficulty slot, and must have one entry each for easy, normal and hard. A missing or short list is read past its end. The rating is read only while [the target's strength is at or below `IonCannonDamage`](/systems/superweapons/#the-computers-use). A structure above that figure keeps the flat structure rating of 3.
