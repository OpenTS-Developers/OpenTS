---
key: C4
summary: Makes a soldier arm a structure with a demolition charge instead of shooting at it.
see_also: ["system:capture"]
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

Setting this also forces [`Infiltrate=yes`](/keys/infiltrate/) on, in the same pass and after that key has been read, so a section that sets `C4=yes` cannot write `Infiltrate=no`.

The soldier takes the demolitions cursor over a non-allied [`Repairable=yes`](/keys/repairable/) structure and an ordinary attack cursor over one without it, which is the split between shooting a barrel and bombing a war factory. [Arming a structure](/systems/capture/#arming-a-structure) covers what the charge then does; the saboteur survives it.

The [`C4` ability](/systems/veterancy/#abilities) reaches the same cursor and the same computer-side mission conversions. A veteran or elite type that holds it therefore behaves as a saboteur without the setting. Both exempt an infantry from the rule that kills anything finishing a move on rock or water.
