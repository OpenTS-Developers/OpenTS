The projectile is exempt from the one obstacle test that reads it: a [`High=yes`](/keys/high/#scope-overlaytype) overlay does not force it to explode as it passes over. Nothing else in flight, damage or targeting reads the setting, so it neither changes the projectile's flight height nor lets it clear terrain.
