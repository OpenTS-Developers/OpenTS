`Rotor4MovingRate` sets the frame count for rotor 4 to complete a 360-degree turn while moving or hovering. `0` stops its moving rotation. [Aircraft rotors](/systems/aircraft-rotors/#voxel-artwork) explains voxel rotor timing.
