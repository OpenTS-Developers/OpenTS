---
key: Scenario
summary: The mission a campaign opens with, or the mission number from which a music track may be picked.
---

This key means different things in its two scopes. In a campaign's own section of `battle.ini` it names the [map the campaign opens with](/keys/scenario/#scope-campaign). In a `theme.ini` score it is the [mission number the playlist must reach before the score plays](/keys/scenario/#scope-themes).
