---
key: Suffix
summary: Names a theater's artwork extension; on a country it is parsed and never used.
---

On a [theater](/keys/suffix/#scope-theater) the value is the text after the dot in the theater's artwork filenames, and the dot itself is not written: the engine appends it, so `Suffix=DES` names `DES.MIX` and `ISODES.PAL`. On a [country](/keys/suffix/#scope-housetype) the value is stored and never used.
