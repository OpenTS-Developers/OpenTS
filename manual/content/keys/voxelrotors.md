---
key: VoxelRotors
summary: Selects voxel models for an aircraft's rotors.
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`VoxelRotors=yes` selects VXL models instead of custom SHP files for an aircraft's rotors. If the selected VXL is missing or fails to load, the engine uses the built-in SHP rotor. [Aircraft rotors](/systems/aircraft-rotors/#voxel-artwork) explains the model keys and rotation.
