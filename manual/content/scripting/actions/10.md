---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_MOVIE
title: Play Movie...
summary: Hands the whole screen over to a movie and returns the mission when it finishes.
caveats:
  - Only a campaign game shows the movie unless the launch file asked for movies with `PlayMoviesInMultiplayer`. Otherwise a skirmish or any multiplayer session reaches the action, passes over the movie without a word, and carries on with the rest of the trigger's actions.
  - A movie that cannot be found, cannot be opened, or is too small to be shown is passed over just as silently.
  - The escape key cuts the movie short and the mission resumes from that point. No other key does. In a game against other machines every player has to press it; the multiplayer movies page owns that vote.
  - The screen is filled black before the movie and again after it, and the whole display is redrawn once the mission comes back.

## What happens to the music

The action names no music track of its own, so nothing is queued behind the movie, and nothing is faded or stopped on the action's account.

The music is not stopped for the movie either. A movie's soundtrack opens an audio stream of its own beside the track already playing, and the game's audio output, opened once when the game starts, is not rebuilt around a movie. A track playing when the movie opens plays on through it.
