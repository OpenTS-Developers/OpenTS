---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_INGAME_MOVIE
title: Play Ingame Movie...
summary: Plays a movie a frame at a time inside the radar pane while the mission carries on around it.
caveats:
  - The movie is not drawn over the tactical view. The radar pane is given over to it, and the radar returns to the mode it was showing once the movie has run out.
  - EVA announces an incoming transmission as the radar switches over, and the movie holds its first frame until she has finished. Music, sound effects and speech are then halved in volume, and all three are put back to what they were once the last queued movie has finished.
  - Only a campaign game plays the movie unless the launch file asked for movies. Otherwise a skirmish or any multiplayer session passes over it without a word.
  - Movies asked for while one is already running are held in order and played one after another, and the radar only goes back to its previous mode once the last of them has finished.
