---
type: action
id: TACTION_DISABLE_SPEECH
title: Disable Speech
summary: Silences EVA's general voice lines and leaves the rest of her speech audible.
caveats:
  - The block is decided by the sample a line names, not by what asked for the line. A line whose sample name begins with `00-` or `01-` is dropped, and a line whose sample name begins with anything else is spoken exactly as it would have been.
  - Whatever is being spoken at the moment plays out. Only lines asked for afterward are affected.
  - The setting stands until something turns speech back on. It is turned back on as each scenario is set up, and it is stored in a saved game. It is one setting for the running game, and no house or player has one of its own.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ENABLE_SPEECH
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_PLAY_SPEECH
---
