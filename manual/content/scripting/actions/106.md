---
type: action
id: TACTION_GIVE_CREDITS
title: Give Credits...
summary: Adds the given number of credits to a house, or takes that many away when the number is negative.
valid_values:
  - A live house selected by a [house number](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses), and a whole number of credits.
caveats:
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing.
  - A negative amount is spent like a purchase, so the house's credits stop at zero and the Tiberium held in its silos and refineries covers the rest.
  - The amount must fit between `-2147483647` and `2147483647`. An addition that would raise the house's credits above `2147483647` is refused.
related:
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
  - type: event
    id: TEVENT_CREDITS
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
Bonus=1,106,0,0,5000,0,0,0, ; the house at index 0 gains 5000 credits
Fine=1,106,0,0,-2000,0,0,0, ; and loses 2000
```

The amount occupies the first of the four fields that follow the house.
