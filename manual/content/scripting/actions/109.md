---
type: action
id: TACTION_CREATE_BUILDING_AT
title: Create Building At...
summary: Places a building of the given type for the given house at the action's waypoint.
valid_values:
  - A house in play, a BuildingType by its index in the loaded `[BuildingTypes]` list, a force flag, and a waypoint the map has placed.
caveats:
  - The house is the first parameter, the BuildingType index the second and the force flag the third. The waypoint is the last field of the line, and a line that omits it names waypoint `A`.
  - With the flag above zero the building is created the way the scenario loader creates one, so the placement rules are not consulted and it stands complete without a build-up. Without the flag the building is refused wherever its footprint cannot be occupied, and the action does nothing. Walls still need an occupiable cell, and upgrades need a matching structure with room for the upgrade, even with forced placement.
  - A house nobody plays, a spawn house nobody holds, an index outside the list, or a waypoint the map has not placed leaves the action doing nothing.
related:
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
---

```ini title="map file"
[Waypoints]
1=45060 ; waypoint 1 serializes as the letter label B

[Actions]
Outpost=1,109,0,0,12,1,0,0,B ; the house at index 0 receives BuildingType 12 at waypoint B, forced
```
