---
type: action
id: TACTION_HOUSE_DESTROY_ALL
title: Destroy all of...
summary: Destroys everything the given house owns and marks the house defeated.
caveats:
  - The house is defeated the multiplayer way, in a campaign too. Its defeat is announced, and when it is the player's own house the player is left watching the map. Once one house that is not passive remains, the game ends with a win or a loss for the player.
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LOSE
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
---

Vehicles are destroyed first so that their crews die with them, then aircraft, infantry and structures, each taking damage at point-blank range until it is gone.
