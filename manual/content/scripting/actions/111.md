---
type: action
id: TACTION_MAKE_ELITE
title: Make Elite
summary: Promotes every infantry, vehicle, aircraft and structure the trigger is attached to, to elite.
caveats:
  - Only an object standing on the map is promoted; one carried inside another or still being placed is left alone.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_DELETE_OBJECT
---

The promotion is the one a unit earns by fighting, so the elite bonuses and the elite chevrons follow from it.
