---
type: action
id: TACTION_MAKE_ALLY_ONE_WAY
title: Make Ally (One-Way)...
summary: Allies the trigger's house with the given house without the reverse alliance.
caveats:
  - The alliance is made the way the scenario loader makes one, so the limits on alliances do not apply. A defeated house, an observer and a last remaining enemy are all accepted, and no message or announcement follows.
  - The named house keeps its own stance and its units keep attacking. Use the reverse action for it, or Make ally for both sides at once.
  - A house nobody plays, a spawn house nobody holds, or the trigger's own house leaves the action doing nothing.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_MAKE_ALLY
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_MAKE_ENEMY_ONE_WAY
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
Truce=1,117,0,1,0,0,0,0, ; the trigger's house allies with the house at index 1
```
