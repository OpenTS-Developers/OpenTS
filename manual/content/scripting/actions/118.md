---
type: action
id: TACTION_MAKE_ENEMY_ONE_WAY
title: Make Enemy (One-Way)...
summary: Has the trigger's house declare war on the given house.
caveats:
  - When the trigger's house is allied with the named house, it breaks that alliance and any reverse alliance. No message or announcement follows.
  - If only the named house is allied with the trigger's house, that reverse alliance is left intact.
  - In a skirmish or multiplayer game a passive house, such as Neutral, neither declares war nor has it declared on it.
  - A house nobody plays, a spawn house nobody holds, or the trigger's own house leaves the action doing nothing.
related:

    id: TACTION_MAKE_ENEMY

    id: TACTION_MAKE_ALLY_ONE_WAY

    id: scenario-objects
