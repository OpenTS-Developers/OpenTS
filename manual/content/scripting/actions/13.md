---
type: action
id: TACTION_AUTOCREATE
title: Autocreate Begins...
summary: Marks the house named as alerted, which nothing reads.
valid_values:
  - A house available to the scenario. Every house behaves the same way, because nothing reads what is stored.
caveats:
  - No team is created and no house builds anything it would not have built without the action. The mark is written and no path that runs reads it back.
  - The routine that once raised autocreate teams from the mark is not compiled into the game, and the team pass that survives it does not read the mark either.
  - One other route sets the same mark. A computer house whose IQ reaches the production threshold marks itself alerted on its next AI pass, and that costs it nothing either.
  - Naming no house, or a country that no house in the scenario plays, leaves the house unresolved and the action does nothing.

`Autocreate=yes` on a TeamType is a separate setting that shares the word and nothing else. It selects which of an object's two recruitable states a team of that type reads when it recruits, and no part of it is switched on or off by this action. What decides whether a house raises teams is [the AI-trigger switch and the ratio behind it](/systems/ai-team-production/#when-the-pass-runs).
