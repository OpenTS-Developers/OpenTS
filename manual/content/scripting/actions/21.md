---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_SPEECH
title: Play speech...
summary: Queues one EVA line to be spoken after the lines already playing and waiting.
caveats:
  - Two Play speech actions in the same trigger both play, one after the other, so long as they name different lines.
  - Asking for a line that is already playing or already waiting does nothing.
  - Nothing is spoken while the speech volume is at zero.
  - Disabling speech does not silence every line. Which ones it silences is settled by the sample the line names, not by the action that asked for it.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_DISABLE_SPEECH
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ENABLE_SPEECH
---
