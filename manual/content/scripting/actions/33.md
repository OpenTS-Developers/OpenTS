---
type: action
id: TACTION_1_SPECIAL
title: Add 1-time special weapon...
summary: Gives the trigger's house one charged shot of a superweapon, and its cameo where that house is the player's.
valid_values:
  - The position of a superweapon in the declared list, which is the order in which `[SuperWeaponTypes]` names its sections. The first section declared is `0`. A position past the end of that list is not checked before it is read.
caveats:
  - The weapon goes to the house that owns the trigger.
  - A house that already holds the weapon is left exactly as it was. The action cannot turn a weapon a structure granted into a one-time one, and it cannot add a second shot to a one-time weapon still in hand.
  - The grant is silent. Neither the charging nor the ready announcement plays, whichever house receives it.
related:
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_FULL_SPECIAL
  - type: system
    id: sidebar
---
