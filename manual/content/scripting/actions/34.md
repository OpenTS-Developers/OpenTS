---
type: action
id: TACTION_FULL_SPECIAL
title: Add repeating special weapon...
summary: Gives the trigger's house a superweapon that recharges for the rest of the mission and needs no structure behind it.
valid_values:
  - The position of a superweapon in the declared list, where the first section declared is `0`. A position past the end of that list is not checked before it is read.
caveats:
  - The weapon goes to the house that owns the trigger.
  - The tie to a structure is cut whether or not the grant itself did anything. Running this on a house that already holds the weapon from a structure leaves it holding the weapon after that structure is sold or destroyed.
  - The weapon arrives with no charging announcement, whichever house receives it.
  - The ready announcement still plays for the local player once the weapon finishes charging.
related:
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_1_SPECIAL
  - type: system
    id: sidebar
---
