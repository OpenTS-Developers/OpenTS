type: action
id: TACTION_DO_EXPLOSION
title: Do Explosion At...
summary: Detonates a weapon's damage and warhead at a waypoint, with no firer, projectile or firing effects.
valid_values:
  - A position in the rules weapon list, counted from `0`, and a scenario waypoint.
caveats:
  - The weapon is identified by position rather than by name, so reordering, adding to or removing from the rules `[Weapons]` list shifts every number already stored in a map. A weapon that list leaves out is numbered where it is first named instead.
  - A position at or beyond the end of the weapon list detonates nothing.
  - No firer is recorded, so the weapon's own range, rate of fire, projectile, sounds and firing animation play no part, no house is angered or awarded points, and nothing gains veterancy from a kill.
  - An object destroyed with no firer behind the blast leaves several of the trigger events on its own tag unsprung.

## What is detonated

Only two figures are taken from the weapon: its `Damage` and its `Warhead`. Everything else about the weapon is ignored, and the detonation happens at the waypoint's cell at ground height, raised by the bridge height where a bridge covers or has covered that cell.

Three things then follow from those two figures, in this order:

1. The explosion animation the warhead selects for that amount of damage on that terrain is created, when the warhead selects one at all.
2. The lighting flash for that damage and warhead is applied.
3. The damage is dealt, on the same terms as any other blast from that warhead.

A weapon whose ID is `empulseweapon`, in any letter case, also throws [an EM pulse](/systems/emp-pulse/) after the blast.

The weapon is only a carrier for those two figures, but it still needs a position to be counted. The [`[Weapons]` list](/formats/rules-registries/) sets those positions in the order it writes them, so a weapon section written for this action alone needs a line there; one nothing names at all is never registered.

## What the destroyed object springs

An object destroyed with no firer behind the blast does not spring [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/) on its own tag, nor [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Discovered by player](/mapping/events/tevent-discovered/). [Destroyed by anything](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any/) and its no-infiltrate form still spring.
