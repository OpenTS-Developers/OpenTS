---
type: action
id: TACTION_ALL_HUNT
title: All to Hunt...
summary: Pulls every infantry, vehicle and aircraft of the house named out of its team and puts it on the hunt mission.
valid_values:
  - A house available to the scenario. It is matched by country against the houses the scenario plays.
caveats:
  - Each object is taken out of whatever team it belonged to first, so a team of that house is left with no members and the members lose whatever mission it had them on.
  - Only infantry, vehicles and aircraft that are on the map are reached. Structures are never touched, and neither is anything still in limbo or not yet placed.
  - The house is marked as hunting for the rest of the scenario, and the mark outlasts the sweep. While it stands, and while the house has a designated enemy, every candidate target that belongs to some other house is scored down to the lowest value a target can hold. The house's objects then converge on that one opponent and treat everything else as a last resort.
  - A country that no house in the scenario plays leaves the house unresolved and the action does nothing.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_FIRE_SALE
  - type: system
    id: target-selection
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
---

The order is given once, so an object that is later given something else to do keeps that new order; nothing puts it back on the hunt. The mark left on the house is the lasting part, and it changes how everything the house owns picks targets from then on, including objects built long afterward and objects that were never swept.
