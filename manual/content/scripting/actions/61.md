---
type: action
id: TACTION_TURN_OFF_ATTACHED
title: Turn off building
summary: Takes every structure whose tag is attached to this trigger out of service.
caveats:
  - The action sweeps every structure in the scenario and switches off each one whose tag is attached to the same trigger; it is not limited to the structure that sprang it. A structure in limbo, or one not yet put down on the map, is passed over.
  - Only a structure currently switched on is reached, and the switch itself asks that the type's `Power=` be negative or that it be `Powered=yes`. A tagged structure that is neither is left running.
  - Every structure the action does switch off announces itself separately to a house under player control.
