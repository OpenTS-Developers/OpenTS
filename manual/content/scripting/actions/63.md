type: action
id: TACTION_DAMAGE
title: Apply 100 damage at...
summary: Stages five separate 100-point blasts around a waypoint and brings down a bridge span there.
valid_values:
  - A scenario waypoint. The action reads it from the waypoint number the line holds, not from the waypoint name at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - The editor text describes one blast of 100 damage. Five are staged, each of 100 in its own right, so anything standing at the waypoint takes all five with only the falloff between them to tell them apart.
  - The warhead is `C4Warhead` from the rules rather than a fixed high-explosive one, so retuning that setting retunes this action.
  - A bridge span over the waypoint is brought down as well, whether or not the blasts found anything to damage.
  - The number field is where the engine writes this waypoint as well as where it reads it, so the number a line holds there survives the engine writing the scenario out again. The name at the end of the line is written from a separate field. That is the one an editor is prompted to fill in, and the one the actions that read a waypoint from the end of the line work from.
  - No firer is recorded, so the blast has the same consequences as a scripted explosion — no points, no anger, no veterancy, and no Destroyed by any house event on what the blasts destroy.

## The five blasts

The first blast is centered on the waypoint. The other four are offset a third of a cell along each axis, one toward each diagonal, and each deals its own 100 points through the same warhead. Every blast gathers what stands in its own cell and the eight around it, and scales the damage by distance. The five overlap heavily, so an object at the waypoint is hit by all of them in the same frame.

The height is the cell's own ground height, raised by the bridge height where a bridge covers or has covered the cell. A waypoint under a bridge is therefore struck at deck level rather than on the ground beneath it.

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoDamage=1,63,0,12,0,0,0,0,M ; trigger DemoDamage detonates at waypoint 12, whose label is M
```

The number `12` is the waypoint the action acts on. The trailing `M` is the same waypoint written as its label, in the field an editor fills in.

## The bridge

After the blasts, the action tries up to four times to bring down a bridge span in the waypoint's cell, then marks the surrounding area for redraw. The attempt is made on every firing and does not depend on the blasts having found anything; a waypoint with no bridge over it simply has nothing to take down.
