---
type: action
id: TACTION_FORCE_END
title: Force end
summary: Awards the player at this machine the victory where nothing has settled the mission yet, and otherwise leaves the standing verdict alone.
caveats:
  - Nothing about the ending is neutral. A mission that has reached no verdict is won by the player at this machine. In a campaign game the mission-accomplished caption and EVA line play; in any other session the victory caption and line play instead.
  - Unlike the two announce actions, this one leaves the savour delay in place, so the mission runs on for that long before the result is carried out.
  - Where a win or a loss has already been flagged, the action leaves it exactly as it stands and changes neither the verdict nor when it lands.
  - A player already flagged to have everything destroyed is left alone as well, and no verdict is awarded.
related:

    id: TACTION_ANNOUNCE_WIN
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ANNOUNCE_LOSE
  - type: key
    id: SavourDelay
