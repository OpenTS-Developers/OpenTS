## How the group arrives

The members are created as the scenario's own objects are, so nothing is produced, nothing is paid for and the delivery does not wait on a factory. They are gathered into one team, given the veterancy the team type asks for, and filled to capacity where the type says so; anything with room for passengers takes the rest of the group aboard.

The group's destination is the cell the team type's own [`Waypoint`](/keys/waypoint/) names.

Four deliveries are possible, and the group's own composition picks between them.

- A drop pod team of infantry alone falls onto the destination cell.
- A group whose every member burrows arrives underground and surfaces at the destination.
- A group of infantry alone walks out of a structure or transport standing at or beside the destination, where there is one to walk out of.
- Anything else enters at the calculated cell on the receiving house's [`Edge`](/keys/edge/) and drives, walks or flies in from there.

A dropship load is a case of its own, and [`StartingDropships`](/keys/startingdropships/) owns what it carries and what goes wrong when the receiving house is not the player's.

:::caution[A task force that builds nothing delivers nothing]
A member entry is accepted with a count of zero: `0=0,E1` is counted as a member of the force and builds no infantryman. A task force whose entries all read that way therefore creates no object at all, and the action delivers nothing. Both reinforcement actions reach it the same way.
:::
