---
type: action
id: TACTION_BEGIN_AI_TRIGGERS
title: AI triggers begin...
summary: Switches the house named into raising teams from its AI triggers.
valid_values:
  - A house available to the scenario, given as a number on the line. It is matched by country against the houses the scenario plays.
caveats:
  - The switch is not one source of teams among several; it is the only one the team pass has. A house without it raises no team of any kind on its own, and switching it on is what starts the house making teams at all.
  - The switch starts off for every house, and in a campaign game nothing but this action turns it on.
  - The pass is not restricted to computer houses, so this switches a human house into raising teams just as readily.
  - Naming no house does nothing at all. Naming a country that no house in the scenario plays finds no house, and no switch is set. The rest of the trigger's actions run as they would otherwise.
