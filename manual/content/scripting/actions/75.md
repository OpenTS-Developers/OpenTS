---
type: action
id: TACTION_STOP_AI_TRIGGERS
title: AI triggers stop...
summary: Stops the house named raising any further teams.
valid_values:
  - A house available to the scenario, given as a number on the line. It is matched by country against the houses the scenario plays.
caveats:
  - The house raises no team of any kind on its own. The team pass has no other source of suggestions, so its countdown goes on expiring with nothing raised in their place.
  - Teams the house already holds are left alone. They keep their members and go on running their scripts, and the ones that are still short go on recruiting.
  - The switch can be turned back on with the matching action, and nothing else in a campaign game turns it on again by itself.
  - Naming no house does nothing at all. Naming a country that no house in the scenario plays finds no house, and no switch is set. The rest of the trigger's actions run as they would otherwise.
