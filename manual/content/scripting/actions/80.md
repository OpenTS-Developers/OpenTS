---
type: action
id: TACTION_REINFORCEMENTS_SPECIAL
title: Reinforcement (team) [at waypoint]...
summary: Builds one team of the type named and places it at the waypoint given rather than at the team type's own.
valid_values:
  - A team type and a scenario waypoint. The waypoint is read from the name at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - An omitted waypoint field is read as waypoint `A`, so the team is delivered there. A field that is not a waypoint label, such as a number, makes the action do nothing.
  - The members appear at the waypoint rather than traveling in, so the receiving house's edge is not used. The nearest cell they fit in is chosen when the waypoint's own is occupied.
  - A group of infantry alone still walks out of a structure or transport standing at or beside the waypoint where there is one, and a drop pod team of infantry alone still falls onto it.
  - Everything else about the delivery matches the plain reinforcement action, including the script the team is given and what happens to a task force that builds nothing.
related:

    id: TACTION_REINFORCEMENTS

    id: Waypoint

    id: TaskForce

    id: ai-team-execution
