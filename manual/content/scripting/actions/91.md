---
type: action
id: TACTION_GO_BERZERK
title: Go Berzerk
summary: Sends every infantry whose tag is attached to this trigger berserk.
caveats:
  - The editor text names a cyborg. Every infantry is eligible, whatever its type and whatever its strength.
  - The action sweeps every infantry in the scenario and takes each one whose tag is attached to the same trigger; it is not limited to the soldier that sprang it. Vehicles, aircraft and structures are never reached.
  - Setting `[CombatDamage] BerzerkAllowed=no` is no protection. That gate belongs to the route through damage, and this action does not read it.
  - The soldier is not given a mission. The damage route puts it on the guard-area mission as it turns, so a soldier sent berserk by this action and left on a mission that looks for no targets simply carries on as it was.
related:

    id: BerzerkAllowed

    id: target-selection
