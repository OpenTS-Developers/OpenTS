---
type: action
id: TACTION_MULTI_MISSILE
title: Nuke strike...
summary: Flies a cluster missile in from the nearest map edge and detonates it on the waypoint.
caveats:
  - No superweapon, silo, charge or owning house is involved, and nothing is announced. The missile simply appears at the edge cell nearest the target.
  - The projectile, warhead, damage and range all come from the weapon named `MultiLauncher`. The lookup is by that fixed name and the result is used without being checked, so a rules tree that registers no such weapon reads one position before the start of the weapon list.
  - The flight speed is fixed in the engine at 50; the weapon's own `Speed` is not read.
  - The target is raised to deck height only while a bridge still stands over the waypoint's cell.
  - No firer is recorded, so the blast earns no points, stirs no anger, grants no veterancy and springs no destroyed-by-house event on what it kills.
related:

    id: superweapons
