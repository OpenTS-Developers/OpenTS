## Where the offer comes from

The event reaches a tag in two different places. The tag may ride on a map cell, on an object, or on both.

A **cell tag** is offered it as an object reaches the cell's center, under **all of:**

- the object is not fully cloaked;
- the cell has a tag;
- **any of:**
  - the cell is neither under a bridge nor marked as having been under one;
  - the object is on the bridge deck.

An **object's own tag** is offered it the moment infantry arrives at that object. Infantry loading into a transport or entering a structure reaches that point. So does an engineer sent to capture or repair a structure, a spy sent to infiltrate one, a saboteur sent to demolish one, and a thief sent to steal a vehicle. In every one of those the offer names the arriving infantry, so the house tested is that infantry's owner.

## Changing hands offers it as well

Every route that moves an object to a new owner offers the event on whatever tag the object holds at the time. That covers an engineer or a thief taking it, the Change House action, and a team's change-house mission. The offer names the object being taken rather than anything that entered it. Ownership is not moved across until afterwards, so the house the test compares is the one losing the object, not the one taking it.

Where infantry did the taking, the ownership offer follows the arrival offers that named the infantry; where an action or a team mission did it, it is the only offer. An engineer that captures a structure makes two arrival offers rather than one, so a persistent trigger there fires twice before the ownership offer arrives.

A trigger reading "Entered by GDI" on a Nod structure is satisfied when a GDI engineer walks in. On the same structure, a trigger reading "Entered by Nod" is satisfied at the same moment by the ownership offer. A volatile trigger that fired on the first offer refuses the second. Where an engineer holds a tag its trigger type marks as transferable, that tag replaces the structure's own before the ownership offer is made. The ownership offer then goes to the replacement instead.
