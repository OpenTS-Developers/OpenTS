## What registers a structure

Two separate paths write the record the event reads, and only one of them is production.

A structure finished by a factory registers its type against the house that built it, which is the behavior the event's name describes. A structure also registers its type whenever a house discovers it, whoever discovers it and wherever the structure came from; the record written is always the owner's. In a campaign, uncovering an enemy base therefore writes that enemy house's record, one structure at a time, in the order the player finds them. Outside a campaign, every human player discovers each structure as it is placed, so the structure registers then.

The record survives whatever satisfied it, and the event is remembered once it is satisfied. A persistent tag holding it therefore fires on every offer from the first one that matches onward. Watching for a structure the house owns, rather than for the last one it registered, is what [Building exists...](/mapping/events/tevent-building-exists/) does instead.
