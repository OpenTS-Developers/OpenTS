## What the exception covers

Walking a soldier into a structure or onto a vehicle it is aimed at ends with the soldier deleted, whether it captured anything, restored anything, spied on anything or [did nothing at all](/systems/capture/#the-soldier-is-consumed). [Destroyed by anything](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any/) is sprung on the soldier's own tag on the way out, and this event is not.
Everything else the pair sees is identical, and it is wider than a destruction. [A change of ownership is booked as a kill](/systems/capture/#what-changes-hands) and so is a structure sold back, and both forms answer to either. The one thing neither form sees afterward is the vehicle a thief drove off in. A vehicle holding a tag [transferred to it that way](/systems/capture/#the-vehicle-branch) springs no destruction event when it is later destroyed.
