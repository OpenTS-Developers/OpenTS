## What counts besides a destruction

The event is offered wherever the game books the object as a kill, and that covers more moments than a destruction. [A change of ownership is booked as one](/systems/capture/#what-changes-hands). An engineer taking a structure or a thief taking a vehicle springs the event on whatever tag the object is holding at that moment. Selling a structure back does the same. None of those credits an attacker, which is exactly what keeps them away from [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/).

Two removals reach the event through the removed object's own tag rather than a kill. A soldier that walks into a structure, or that takes over a vehicle, [springs it as the last step before it is deleted](/systems/capture/#the-vehicle-branch), and that is the moment [the form with the exception](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any-x/) is written to avoid. Infantry and aircraft caught on ice that gives way spring it as they are removed, and both forms see that one alike. A vehicle there goes under instead unless its [`MovementZone`](/keys/movementzone/) lets it swim.
