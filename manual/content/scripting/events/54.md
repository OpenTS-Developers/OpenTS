title: Enemy In Spotlight... (repeating)
summary: Fires while a sweeping beam on the tagged structure has a non-allied soldier or vehicle inside its detection radius, and is never marked off once it has fired.
caveats:
  - The two spotlight events run no test of their own. Both are sprung together on the same find, so they answer identically to every offer they are given.
  - The one difference is that this form is never marked off as having happened. It is examined afresh on every later offer, while the plain form stays satisfied.
  - Nothing is marked off on a volatile or a semi-persistent tag. Both forms behave alike there, so the choice between them changes nothing.

[Enemy In Spotlight...](/mapping/events/tevent-enemy-in-spotlight/) covers what the scan takes and which beams reach it, and all of it applies to this form unchanged. What separates the two is [whether a satisfied event is remembered](/systems/trigger-springing/#remembering-a-satisfied-event), and this is the form that is not remembered.

The difference shows on a [persistent tag](/systems/trigger-springing/#tag-lifetimes) whose trigger pairs the event with something else. With the plain form, one intruder settles the spotlight term for the rest of the scenario. The trigger then waits only on its other events. With this form the beam has to have an intruder in view on the very [offer](/systems/trigger-springing/#where-a-tag-is-offered-an-event) that satisfies the last of them.
