## Picking what to follow

The choice is remade every turn. Every vehicle and every infantry in the scenario is measured against one member of the team, and the nearest one that clears **all of** these becomes [the team's center](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-teams-center):

- it is alive, active and out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo);
- its house is not the team's own house;
- its house is allied to the team's house;
- it is not a member of this team.

An allied house is required, and the team's own house is excluded by name, so this line follows somebody else's objects and never the owner's. Where the object picked is inside a tunnel, the center becomes that tunnel's exit cell instead of the object, so the team gathers at the mouth rather than chasing a position it cannot reach.

## Following and joining in

The center is then made the team's destination, and the members are walked toward it by [the move coordinator](/systems/ai-team-execution/#moving), on the same terms as an ordinary move. That coordinator is what eventually ends the line, since it is the only thing here that moves the team on.

The team also takes its fights from the object it is following. Each member that is in play and holds a target other than the followed object's target drops it. Each member that is armed, standing still and on Guard is then sent to attack whatever the followed object is aiming at.
