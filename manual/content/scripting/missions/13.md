id: TMISSION_IDLE_ANIM
title: "Idle Anim..."
summary: "Has every infantry member play the numbered idle animation."
valid_values:
  - "`1` plays the type's second idle animation."
  - "Any other number plays its first, so `0` and `7` come to the same thing. Only infantry have idle animations to play, so the line does nothing to a vehicle or an aircraft."
caveats:
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."
