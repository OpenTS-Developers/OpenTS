## What the team is aimed at

On the turn the line starts, the team's mission target becomes the waypoint's cell. The line then looks for an object standing on that cell. Where it finds one, that object becomes the target for good. A structure caught there is followed as an object from that point on, and the cell is not checked again.

Finding nothing there, the line does nothing whatever and repeats the same look on the next turn. Nothing else about the team changes and nothing advances it.

## Walking in rather than shooting

The team is coordinated as an attack, with one exception written into that coordination for this mission alone. An infantry member whose type is [`Infiltrate=yes`](/keys/infiltrate/) is put on the capture mission aimed at the target; every other member is put on attack.

What happens when such a soldier arrives is settled at the structure by the flags the soldier has: [`Engineer=yes`](/keys/engineer/#scope-infantrytype) takes or repairs it, [`Agent=yes`](/keys/agent/) infiltrates it, and [anything else is simply consumed](/systems/capture/#the-soldier-is-consumed). `Engineer=yes` and [`C4=yes`](/keys/c4/) both force `Infiltrate=yes` on, so an engineer team reaches the capture branch without the setting being written; a spy only reaches it where its section sets `Infiltrate=yes` explicitly, and shoots at the structure otherwise.
