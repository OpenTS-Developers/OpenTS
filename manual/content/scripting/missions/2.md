---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_BERZERK
title: Go Berzerk
summary: Sends the team's infantry berserk and moves straight on to the next line.
caveats:
  - Only infantry answer it. Every member is called on, but a vehicle or an aircraft has nothing to do with the call.
  - The advance is raised on the same turn, so the line costs the script no time of its own.
  - Nothing ever takes a soldier back out of it, and no member is given a mission to go with it.
  - Setting `[CombatDamage] BerzerkAllowed=no` does not stop it, and does not stop the trigger action either.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_GO_BERZERK
---

Every member is walked and sent berserk, and the [script step](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-script-cursor) moves on as soon as the walk is done. A berserk soldier attacks whatever it finds nearby with no regard for who owns it, so the line only helps where the team already stands among objects it should attack.

The turn is one way. Nothing clears the state again, and the line hands out no mission alongside it, so a team whose next line looks for no targets keeps its old orders, and its soldiers attack only what comes within reach. [Go Berzerk](/mapping/actions/taction-go-berzerk/) as a trigger action is the same effect aimed at the infantry carrying a tag rather than at a team, and that page owns what being berserk amounts to.
