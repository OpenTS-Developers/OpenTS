caveats:
  - "The substitute is looked for only where the waypoint lies inside the playable area. A move aimed at a waypoint outside it is left aimed there, whether or not the leading member could stand on it. That is how a team is sent off the map."
  - "The substitute is chosen for the leading member rather than for the slowest or the widest, and the leading member is simply the first one taking part."
  - "Where no substitute can be found the team is left with no destination at all and sits on this line doing nothing."
  - "The line raises nothing itself. The team takes the next line once every member has arrived and come to a stop, so one member that cannot get there holds the line open."
