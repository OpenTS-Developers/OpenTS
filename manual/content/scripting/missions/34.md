---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_CENTER_VIEWPOINT
title: Center view on team (speed)...
summary: Pans the tactical view onto the team at one of five fixed rates, without waiting for it to arrive.
valid_values:
  - One of the five camera speeds. The figure is used as a position in a table of five rates with no range check.
caveats:
  - The line does not wait for the pan. It raises the team's advance on the turn it runs, so the next line starts while the view is still traveling.
  - The destination is fixed when the line runs. The view goes to where the team was on that turn and does not follow it afterward.
  - A team with no center recorded faults here. The line reads the center without testing that one was found, and a team holding no member that is out of limbo and has entered the playable area has none.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_CENTER_VIEWPOINT
  - type: enum
    id: ScrollSpeedType
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
---

The destination is [the team's center](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-teams-center) at its ground height, raised by the bridge height where a bridge covers or has covered that cell. The center is a reference to a cell or to one member rather than to the group as a whole. The view therefore arrives on that one point rather than on the middle of a spread-out team.

The pan itself is the same routine [Center Camera at Waypoint...](/mapping/actions/taction-center-viewpoint/) uses, at the same five rates and with the same lack of a range check on the figure. That page owns how the rates behave, what happens to a player scrolling during the pan, and what a second pan does to one already under way.
