title: "Move to Cell..."
summary: "Sends the team to the cell the number decodes to."
caveats:
  - "The number is split into a column and a row on a width of 1,000 cells when the team first acts on the line. A scenario that declares `NewINIFormat` below `4` states the number in the older width of 128 instead, and that value is converted to the 1,000-wide form as the line is read."
  - "A number that decodes to a cell outside the playfield leaves the line without a target, and the team goes nowhere."
  - "No test is made that the cell is reachable, or that anything can stand there."
  - "The line raises nothing itself. The team takes the next line once every member has arrived and come to a stop, so one member that cannot get there holds the line open."
