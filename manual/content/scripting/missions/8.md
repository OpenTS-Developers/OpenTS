---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_UNLOAD
title: "Unload..."
summary: "Empties every member that is carrying passengers, then keeps or releases transports and passengers according to the chosen split."
valid_values:
  - "`0`: no member is released from the team."
  - "`1`: the members that are not the transport are released from the team."
  - "`2`: the transport is released from the team."
  - "`3`: the transport and the members that are not the transport are all released."
  - "A number outside `0` through `3` releases nobody, the same as `0`."
caveats:
  - "The split is applied only on the turn the last member finishes unloading, so a team interrupted part way through keeps everybody."
  - "TransportsReturnOnUnload=yes overrides the split for the transports. Each one is released from the team whatever the split says, and sent back to where it was standing when the move began."
  - "The line holds the team until no member is still carrying anybody."
---
